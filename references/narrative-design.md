# Design the argument, then write the paper

Adapted from Best-Paper-Writing-Skill b9eff99 (CC BY 4.0); see
[provenance and local changes](bestpaper/README.md).

Use by default for full drafts and substantial narrative revision. A local edit
uses only the relevant claim/paragraph; preserve an effective existing argument.
The parent's shared writing standard and scientific boundaries remain controlling.

## 1. Recover the research and choose the center

Read the author-designated materials before asking for inputs. Extract the
question, available capabilities, remaining difficulty, actual construction or
finding, closest work and decisive evidence. A supplied abstract is one source,
not a license to infer the missing method, references or experiments.
Ask only for a consequential missing fact, grouping related questions; continue
supported sections. Do not require an intake form or approval of an outline.

Privately state the central contribution, the unresolved question/trade-off it
addresses, the observation or rationale behind it and its strongest evidence.
An idea name is optional and should preserve the author's terminology; do not
invent a slogan or require it in the title. A consequence for the field belongs
only where evidence supports it. A theory or resource paper need not have a
headline numerical improvement.

## 2. Choose a route and calibrate promises

Use [research-routes.md](research-routes.md) to choose a primary contribution
route and necessary secondary route. Name the actual tension: competing goals,
missing capability, costly alternative, unexplained phenomenon, open question
or challenged belief. These are diagnostic choices, not required conflict.

Use the single [evidence map](evidence-workflow.md) and
[claim-calibration.md](claim-calibration.md) to connect each substantial claim
to method/construction/proof, results and necessary conditions. Identify what
earns headline, supporting or table-only space. Map many-to-many where needed.
An unsupported promise is narrowed to what is established or kept as an explicit
query in an unfinished draft; it is not repaired with a fabricated experiment.

## 3. Give paragraphs and artifacts jobs

Sketch the central point of each paragraph and what it enables the reader to
understand next. Establish the problem's actual tension before explaining its
resolution. Credit the closest work's specific capability before identifying
what remains. State observations directly; distinguish a proposed explanation
from a finding. Do not force all introductions into an identical order.

Track where each claim is developed. Merge passages that do the same job, but
allow purposeful recurrence: an abstract summarizes, an introduction promises,
a result establishes and a conclusion interprets the same finding at different
levels. A claim is not forbidden from appearing in more than one section.
One central point per paragraph can include explanation, comparison and evidence;
it does not mean one bare fact per sentence.

If several figures are planned, extend the same map with each artifact's role,
claim, material and status using [figure-workflow.md](figure-workflow.md). A table
may establish a result more clearly than a figure; a proof may need neither.

## 4. Draft by scientific dependency

Follow the section owners in SKILL.md. When source material is disorganized,
stabilize method/results and artifact roles before finalizing introduction and
abstract promises. This is a useful dependency order, not a mandatory restart of
an already coherent manuscript. Explain component relations, not parallel names.

Use a few function-matched [exemplars](bestpaper/README.md) only when they help
solve a specific organization/rhythm problem. Learn their information sequence;
do not transplant sentences, scientific claims or experimental strength. Draft
in the authors' voice; keep analysis of supplied materials outside paper prose.

## 5. Read backwards from evidence and forwards as a reader

Reverse-check every major promise against its evidence and scope. Then read
forward: does the problem motivate the actual idea, does the method explain the
operation, do the results answer the question, and does the ending convey the
supported value? Check the role of short pivots, connector dependence and empty
significance clauses in context. Do not flatten distinctive but effective prose
to match a corpus percentile.

Use existing scientific, citation, lint and artifact QA gates. Deliver finished
requested prose and a compact audit/gap record; reuse the project's existing
notes rather than requiring separate brief, claim-map and report files.
